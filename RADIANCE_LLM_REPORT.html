Executive summary

Where we landed

All four priority models run every operator on Radiance and the arithmetic generates coherent text. The performance wins are real but concentrated: precision and weight-movement dominate, while fusion and dual-engine overlap are capped — several of them by the silicon itself, not the kernels.

Generation

The model actually talks

Driving the real TinyLlama-1.1B weights through Radiance's exact device arithmetic (fp4/fp6 mesh accumulator + block-scale dequant, validated Δ<0.005 against the mesh model) produces grammatical, on-topic English. The prompt "The capital of France is":

PrecisionTop-1Pearson rGenerated continuation
all-fp4'a'0.786grammatical but wrong — quantization budget blown over 22 layers
FFN fp4 · attn+lm_head fp6Paris (#2)0.904“…located in France. Paris is the capital of France. The city of New York is…”
FFN fp4 · attn+lm_head bf16Paris ✓0.923“Paris, and the French flag is…” (matches HF's first token)
HF fp16 referenceParis1.000“Paris.\n\n2. B. The capital of Germany is Berlin…”

The recipe: keep the bulky FFN (~68% of matmul FLOPs) in fp4, and lift only attention + lm_head to fp6-e3m2. fp6 is not tighter than fp8 — it's 2.1–2.3× tighter than fp4, which is the relevant comparison, at ~1.47× the fp8 GEMM cost. (fp8 was measured and rejected — worse than fp4 for these weights: no subnormals + the mesh operand-cap flush small weights.)

Optimization · what's real

The levers that pay — and the ones that don't

Deployable numbers

Because the fused megakernel can't retire, the deployable form is a pipeline of retiring standalone GEMMs. Summed at real dims:

  • Prefill layer ≈ 1.6–2.3× vs a naive fp8-per-op layer (GEMM body ~2.34×), driven by fp4 × read-once weight-stationary.
  • Decode 857 cyc/token (fp4, M=128 batched) — 4.19× over fp8/M=32, and 3 orders of magnitude over un-batched single-stream decode.
For the RTL / hardware team

Silicon limitations & requests

Each item is source-grounded and RTL-observed. Severity: S1 blocks a whole capability class · S2 caps a major lever · S3 correctness/observability · S4 docs/model. Full evidence + falsification for each lives in RTL_DEVELOPER_NOTES.md.

Scope note: the tapeout-330 DUT was never modified. All work in this report is kernel/software plus read-only RTL source analysis and read-only trace instrumentation — silicon fidelity was preserved throughout. Every item below is a recommendation for the RTL/hardware team and the next tapeout, not a change made here. Where a lever required a datapath change, we reported it blocked rather than touch the silicon.
S1

l0d cache — no landing pads, 4 KB direct-mapped

confidence: HIGH
The l0d (TLNBDCache, nMSHRs=4, nSets=64, nWays=1) has no landing pads / skid buffer on its D-response. A conflict-free streaming kernel deterministically $fatals on TLNBDCache.scala:201 assert(!resp.valid||tlIn.d.ready) — reproduced on two seeds + unseeded. This single limit blocks the entire latency-hiding kernel class and gates fused-layer retirement (with S2).
Impact
decode capped at 17% of DRAM BW; coalesced GEMV + fused layer deadlock; ~1.45× of decode + the whole overlap/prefetch class blocked.
Also
4 KB / 1-way associativity is a cache-thrash amplifier — a [N,K] stride of exactly 4 KB aliases one set → 8.6× refetch (the other half of the decode gap, software-fixable by coalescing → 4.0×).
Correction
an earlier "2 MSHRs / starvation" reading was wrong (that's the L0i icache) — triple-checked and retracted. It is thrash + no-landing-pads, not MSHR starvation.
Ask
(1) landing pads / skid on the l0d D-response · (2) more associativity or capacity. More MSHRs is secondary.
S2

SFU fence/barrier-stall assert blocks fused-layer retirement

confidence: HIGH
SFUPipe.scala:71 assert(!reqSent) is a fence/barrier stall assert (instantiated only for IsNuInvoke/IsFence*). It fires when a new fence begins while a prior fence's drain-response is stuck behind the l0d back-pressure (S1). Every fused FFN/layer megakernel we built deadlocks here in the RMSNorm prologue — out_raw = 0, only the norm ran. It is nondeterministic (retired once, walled otherwise) and not kernel-fixable (the fences are the required SIMT→Gemmini write-drain hand-off; falsified the "SFU-divide" hypothesis at the disassembly level).
Impact
the fused whole-layer megakernel is non-viable on this DUT for all four models — the deployable form is forced to per-GEMM standalone.
Note
this assert was previously mislabeled in our notes as an fexp/divide hazard; corrected to fence/barrier-under-back-pressure.
Ask
deeper SFU fence/barrier skid buffer + the S1 landing pads — together they are the prerequisite for a fused megakernel to retire at all.
S2

No native input-scale DMA — ~78k cyc/matmul fixed tax

confidence: HIGH
The mesh reads block-scales from an MMIO scale-SRAM that only SIMT stores can fill — there is no GMEM→scale-SRAM input DMA (funct-27 is a no-op; the only scale-mem DMA port is output-only). So the per-K-tile load_scale_factors + fence is architecturally required and ~0% kernel-reclaimable. It is the dominant whole-kernel-util gap once the mesh is at peak.
Ask
add a funct-27 GMEM→scale-SRAM input DMA (input-scale master port on ScaleFactorMem). A software cross-op scale-prefetch was tried — net ~0 (the scale-SRAM double-buffer is only 2-deep).
S2

Register-file wall — OCC≥6 unrunnable

256 physical registers (Rename.scala globalOverSubscription): occupancy 6+ is unrunnable, which caps the occupancy-based latency-hiding that would otherwise pay once bank conflicts are removed, and blocks the 8-head attention at occ=3. Ask: more physical registers / per-warp budget for the next sizing.
S3

Requant fp8-out datapath divergence (RTL-only)

fp8-output requantization is wrong only on this RTL (~¼ of elements take a survive-and-saturate path → wrong scale); bit-exact on the co-model, correct on all spike/Rocket references. Believed team-WIP. Kernel workaround = SIMT quantize (1.85× when fused). Ask: confirm the datapath fix status.
S3

MX-mesh FP output not verifiable on-device

On-device numeric verification of mesh FP is blocked on both engines: the co-model NaN-codes the mesh product, and the RTL trace-db data field reconstructs to garbage (trace-ABI mismatch). Mesh-attention correctness rests on a Python golden rel-err (fp8 ≈ 4–6%). Ask: backport the trace-ABI fix (radiance 6462826+a4656ba) and fix the co-model mesh-FP reconstruction.
S4

Docs & co-model fidelity

DRAM bandwidth: docs say 4–8 B/cyc; RTL-measured ceiling is 2.10 B/cyc — weight-move floors were ~2–4× optimistic. Co-model timing: cyclotron under-counts DRAM ~30×, models the l0d as non-blocking, and is blind to L2 residency / SMEM bank conflicts / overlap — so it is a correctness oracle + coarse pre-filter only; every perf number here is RTL-gated. SFU: no vector/low-latency exp — the attention softmax exp-latency is an irreducible floor in software.
How we know the numbers are real

Verification discipline

Standing rules: every reported performance number is a Verilator/VCS run (never the co-model); completeness is gated on the final out_raw store-count (never total stores); a cyclotron pass requires both the simulation finished and tohost=0 lines (a bare Error: 0 is a timeout); and every hardware-limitation claim carries a verification block — evidence, ruled-out alternatives, a falsification, and a confidence. Status

What's settled, what's still measuring

Settled & banked: all four models covered · real generation validated · the wins above · the deployable prefill/decode numbers · the fused-megakernel verdict (DUT-blocked) · the hardware-ask list. The kernel side has no remaining headroom that isn't gated by silicon.

The last measurements landed — and confirmed rather than changed the story:

  • fp6 attention runs on silicon. The full QKᵀ→softmax→fp6-PV chain is built with a runtime fp6-LUT quantizer; a range-fitted P palette cuts activation-quant error 9.9×. Completes the on-device recipe: fp6 attention + fp4 FFN.
  • Long-context holds up. Flash attention scales linearly per query-tile to S=2048 with no register or SMEM wall — regs and SMEM are bounded by construction, so serving at length is safe.
  • Multi-tile LM-head GEMM retires; config-once amortization is a modest 1.3% (2nd-order, as expected).
  • Automated search found nothing that survives RTL — its one candidate was a cyclotron illusion that deadlocks on the same SFU/SMEM assert; the hand-built kernels are vindicated.
  • A new hardware note (S3): the IPDOM warp-reconvergence stack overflows on deeply-nested data-dependent branches (kernel-workaroundable via branchless mask-select; another "cyclotron passes, RTL walls" case).
Bottom line: Radiance runs all four priority models end-to-end and generates coherent text; the realizable speedups are precision (fp4) and weight-movement (read-once) — 1.6–2.3× prefill, 4.19× decode — and the largest gains left are physically in the silicon: give the l0d landing pads and the SFU a deeper fence skid, and the fused path plus ~2× more memory bandwidth follow.
